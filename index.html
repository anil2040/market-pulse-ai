### 2. KEY MOVES
* **Treasury Yield Surge:** Yields jumped across the curve after Fed remarks; the 2-year Treasury yield surged 12 basis points to 4.35%, while the 10-year yield reached 4.72%.
* **Futures Trading:** U.S. futures are mixed—Dow futures up +0.15%, S&P 500 futures up +0.07%, and Nasdaq 100 futures down -0.16%.
* **Sector Performance:** Technology and communications continue to lead weekly gains due to AI tailwinds, while energy underperformed as oil prices stabilized.
* **Commodities:** WTI Crude oil traded flat near $83–$84 per barrel amid easing Middle East risk premiums and new sanction proposals.

### 3. MACRO & NEWS
* **Hawkish Fed at Jackson Hole:** Fed Chair Kevin Warsh stressed that inflation remains uncomfortably high and reaffirmed a "firm and fixed" 2% PCE target, driving market-implied odds of a September rate hike from 35% up to 55%.
* **Inflation & Labor Data:** July Core PCE held sticky at 3.3% YoY. Weekly jobless claims fell to a resilient 203,000 (vs. 210,000 expected), giving the Fed room to focus primarily on curbing inflation.
* **Treasury Liquidity Intervention:** Treasury Secretary Bessent announced plans to double long-dated bond buybacks to at least $4B and is weighing the use of the $950B Treasury General Account (TGA) to cap yield upside.
* **Trade Escalation:** Negotiations broke down between the U.S. and Canada, triggering 50% U.S. tariffs on roughly $20B of Canadian goods, with retaliatory tariffs scheduled for Sept. 8.
* **AI Policy/Legal:** A federal judge ruled the Pentagon illegally designated AI startup Anthropic a supply-chain risk, removing a regulatory cloud over the vendor.

### 4. EARNINGS HIGHLIGHTS
* **NVIDIA:** Beat Q2 revenue and EPS expectations while issuing above-consensus guidance, confirming robust, ongoing demand for AI infrastructure buildouts.
* **Gap Inc. (GPS):** Shares surged over 16% in extended trading after beating Q2 EPS estimates ($0.52 adjusted) and announcing Old Navy CCO Michael Francis will take over as brand CEO in November. Revenue slightly missed estimates ($3.65B).
* **Salesforce & CrowdStrike:** Both software firms reported better-than-expected quarterly results, reinforcing positive tech sentiment.
* **S&P 500 Aggregate:** With 96% of S&P 500 companies reporting, 86% have beaten earnings estimates, with year-over-year earnings growth spanning 10 of 11 sectors.

### 5. MORNING OUTLOOK
Watch for continued rate-sensitivity in high-valuation tech stocks today as markets adjust to elevated rate-hike probability ahead of upcoming CPI and jobs data. As September approaches—historically a seasonally softer month for equities—investors should anticipate near-term consolidation and use short-term pullbacks in high-quality, large-cap equities as buying opportunities.
