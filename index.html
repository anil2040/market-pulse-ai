  4. Corporate profit margins are at record highs while labor's share of national income has collapsed, creating political and consumer resentment that shows up in persistently sour sentiment despite strong absolute earnings; this distributional imbalance is structurally important for long-term investor returns.
  5. Wednesday's CPI report is the week's critical test: headline expected at 3.6% (up from 3.4%), core at 2.5% (up from 2.4%), which would inform whether the Fed delays a potential rate cut from later this month to early December.

  8. OpenAI and Anthropic revenue metrics are opaque and arbitrary: annualized revenue figures multiplied from snapshot periods lack audited standards, obscuring true cost structures and burn rates; the market is treating private-company metrics as public-company earnings, a gap that IPO filings will expose.
